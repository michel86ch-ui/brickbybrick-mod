Mods & maps

Brick by Brick modsFor Brick by Brick: Tidy Up the Small Town! (Steam)

Extra buildings to build brick by brick, and small mods that make building easier. Every download contains the same unmodified BepInEx 5.4.23.5, so the mods work together: unzip one after the other into the game folder and overwrite.

Slots: the Taj Mahal is played in world slot 3, the Notre-Dame in world slot 4, each in a new save there.

Maps

Notre-Dame

26,462 bricks in five construction sites, with a walkable scaffold up to the spire that stays standing. The first design, with the teething troubles of a first attempt. Part of Extra Buildings. Played in world slot 4.

3D preview Screenshots Download (Extra Buildings 1.2.0)

Test buildings

A garage with 191 bricks and a round tower with 311 bricks, the first tries of Extra Buildings. In the same download.

Taj Mahal recommended

40,262 bricks in seven construction sites, on a platform with four minarets, and a scaffold that disappears once the building is complete. The newer of the two maps and the better one: cleaner brickwork, and it builds well from the first course to the last — about 16 hours. Part of Extra Buildings. Played in world slot 3.

Unofficial fan mods, not made by the game's developers. Ladder, SkillPoints, SkillModify and BuildHelper's ghost hand mark a world as practice (no Steam leaderboard score) once they change something in it; the Extra Buildings worlds are separate from the main world.
