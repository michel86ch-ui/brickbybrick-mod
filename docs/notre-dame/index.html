    For comparison

    Simplified model, about 1:3.3 of the original (128 m long, towers 69 m), drawn with the game's real brick shapes. Walls are 0.21 m thick and laid in running bond with 2×1 blocks. Roof tiles lie flat in rows that step in by one cell per layer (45°), like the game's own roofs. Doors and windows are framed like the house in the game: upright "2×1×2 Blocks" as jambs, the same block lying flat above them, "2×2 Window" pieces as sills or as drip caps over the doors. Windows close in a stepped point (one cell per row), in the round choir too; the belfry openings have the same frames with louvers. The portals are open passages with a lintel, a stepped tympanum and an archivolt. The round choir, its roof and the spire are laid in true rings: blocks at slight angles that overlap a little (the game checks no overlap when a brick is set), every other ring tile 5 mm higher against flickering. This was tried out in the game with a round test tower. The drum under the spire uses the curved wedge blocks of the library's round tower (18 per ring). Reach: the game's scaffold lift goes up to 17.05 m and a brick can be set from up to 5 m away, so towers and pinnacles stay within reach. For the spire the mod adds a walkable scaffold (brown, no bricks): a stair tower from the ground in front of the north transept, a bridge at ridge height to a spiral around drum and spire, and from the spiral at tower height a bridge to both bell towers. Still a design, not yet buildable in the game.
